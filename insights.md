# Business Insights – Sales Analysis

## Overall Performance
- Total revenue generated during January 2024 is ₹803,300.
- Sales are concentrated in a small number of high-performing products.

## Product Insights
- A few products contribute disproportionately to total revenue, confirming the Pareto (80–20) principle.
- Several products perform below the overall average revenue, indicating optimization opportunities.

## Category Insights
- The Electronics category dominates sales and revenue contribution.
- Non-electronics categories contribute marginally and may require targeted promotions.

## Regional Insights
- The North region contributes over 30% of total revenue, making it the strongest region.
- Other regions show moderate and inconsistent sales performance.

## Time-Based Insights
- Sales fluctuate across days rather than showing steady growth.
- Peak revenue was recorded in mid-January 2024.
- Day-over-day sales analysis shows both positive and negative changes, indicating demand volatility.
